\section{Complex Linear Algebra and Least Squares}

Write
\[
u=u_R+i u_I,\qquad v=v_R+i v_I,\qquad A=A_R+iA_I
\]
where all real and imaginary parts are real-valued. Then
\[
\tilde u=
\begin{bmatrix}u_R\\u_I\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
\tilde A=
\begin{bmatrix}
A_R&-A_I\\
A_I&A_R
\end{bmatrix}
\]

\textbf{(a)}
The complex inner product is
\[
\langle u,v\rangle
=u^*v
=(u_R^T v_R+u_I^T v_I)
+i(u_R^T v_I-u_I^T v_R)
\]
The real inner product of the stacked vectors is
\[
\langle \tilde u,\tilde v\rangle
=u_R^T v_R+u_I^T v_I
=\operatorname{Re}\langle u,v\rangle
\]

\textbf{(b)}
The norms are the same:
\[
\|\tilde u\|^2
=u_R^T u_R+u_I^T u_I
=\sum_{i=1}^n |u_i|^2
=\|u\|^2
\]
Thus
\[
\|\tilde u\|=\|u\|
\]

\textbf{(c)}
Since
\[
Au=(A_Ru_R-A_Iu_I)+i(A_Iu_R+A_Ru_I)
\]
we have
\[
\widetilde{Au}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
A_R&-A_I\\
A_I&A_R
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
u_R\\u_I
\end{bmatrix}
=\tilde A\tilde u
\]

\textbf{(d)}
The Hermitian conjugate is
\[
A^*=A_R^T-iA_I^T
\]
The real representation of \(A^*\) is
\[
\widetilde{A^*}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
A_R^T&A_I^T\\
-A_I^T&A_R^T
\end{bmatrix}
\]
But
\[
\tilde A^T
=
\begin{bmatrix}
A_R^T&A_I^T\\
-A_I^T&A_R^T
\end{bmatrix}
\]
Therefore
\[
\widetilde{A^*}=\tilde A^T
\]

\textbf{(e)}
The complex least-squares problem
\[
\min_x \|Ax-y\|^2
\]
is equivalent to the real least-squares problem
\[
\min_{\tilde x}\|\tilde A\tilde x-\tilde y\|^2
\]
because \(\widetilde{Ax-y}=\tilde A\tilde x-\tilde y\) and the norm is preserved by the real representation.

If \(A\) is full column rank, then \(\tilde A\) is also full column rank. The real least-squares solution is
\[
\tilde x_{\mathrm{ls}}
=
(\tilde A^T\tilde A)^{-1}\tilde A^T\tilde y
\]
Using the block real representation, this is
\[
\begin{bmatrix}
\operatorname{Re}x_{\mathrm{ls}}\\
\operatorname{Im}x_{\mathrm{ls}}
\end{bmatrix}
=
\left(
\begin{bmatrix}
A_R&-A_I\\
A_I&A_R
\end{bmatrix}^T
\begin{bmatrix}
A_R&-A_I\\
A_I&A_R
\end{bmatrix}
\right)^{-1}
\begin{bmatrix}
A_R&-A_I\\
A_I&A_R
\end{bmatrix}^T
\begin{bmatrix}
y_R\\y_I
\end{bmatrix}
\]
This is the real representation of
\[
x_{\mathrm{ls}}=(A^*A)^{-1}A^*y=A^\dagger y
\]
so \(A^\dagger y\) solves the complex least-squares problem.
